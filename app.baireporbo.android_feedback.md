# **Opportunities for Enhancement for BairePorbo** 

# **App** 

- ASO Optimization 

- Dynamic User Walkthrough

## **1) ASO Optimization** 

#### **Observation:** 

The current app description lacks sufficient keywords and text optimized for App Store Optimization (ASO), which can hinder discoverability in app stores. 

#### **Why It Matters:** 

By incorporating relevant keywords and expanding the description, the app can improve its ranking in search results. This leads to a higher chance of being discovered by potential users, ultimately increasing download rates. 

#### **Possible Enhancements:** 

- **Keyword Integration:** Research and integrate high-traffic keywords related to studying abroad, scholarships, and higher education within the app description. 

- **Expanded Description:** Add more information about features, benefits, and unique selling points, while maintaining an engaging tone to attract users. 

- **User Testimonials:** Include brief user feedback or success stories to establish credibility and relate to potential users.

## **2) Dynamic User Walkthrough** 

#### **Observation:** 

There is currently no dynamic walkthrough for new users to help them navigate the app effectively. 

#### **Why It Matters:** 

Providing a guided tour helps new users understand how to use the app, reduces confusion, and enhances user satisfaction. This can lead to higher retention rates and improved user experience. 

#### **Possible Enhancements:** 

- **Onboarding Tutorial:** Create an interactive onboarding experience that introduces key features and functionalities of the app when a user first installs it. 

- **Tooltips and Pop-ups:** Implement contextual tooltips throughout the app to assist users as they explore different sections. 

- **Skip Option:** Allow users the option to skip the walkthrough if they are already familiar with similar applications. 

## **3) Password Field Visibility Toggle** 

#### **Observation:** 

The password field does not have a visibility toggle icon for users to show or hide their password input. 

3 



#### **Why It Matters:** 

Adding a visibility toggle improves usability and reduces the likelihood of login errors. It provides users with the ability to confirm their password input, promoting a smoother login experience. 

#### **Possible Enhancements:** 

- **Visibility Toggle Icon:** Introduce an icon that toggles the password visibility, located within the password field, making it easy for users to show or hide their password. 

- **User Interface Feedback:** Provide visual feedback (e.g., changing the icon state) when the toggle is activated to signal to users that the change has been acknowledged. 

- **Accessibility Considerations:** Ensure that the toggle is accessible for all users, including those using screen readers. 

## **4) 'Rate Your App' Feature** 

#### **Observation:** 

Currently, there is no option for users to rate the app easily, which can hinder feedback collection and app credibility. 

#### **Why It Matters:** 

A simple "Rate Your App" button in the settings can greatly enhance the collection of ratings and reviews, which is crucial for new apps to build social proof, increase visibility, and improve trust among potential users. 

4 



#### **Possible Enhancements:** 

- **Settings Integration:** Add a "Rate Your App" option in the settings menu for easy access. 

- **Prompt Timing:** Consider implementing prompts at strategic times after user interactions to encourage users to rate the app when they are most satisfied (e.g., after receiving helpful support from the AI mentor). 

- **Incentives:** Optionally, you could offer small incentives for users who leave a rating, such as access to premium features or additional resources.
